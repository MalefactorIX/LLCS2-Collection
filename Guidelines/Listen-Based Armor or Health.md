# What is Listen-Based Armor (LBA)?
LBA was developed as an alternative to repeatedly bashing objects with prims in order to damage them. This greatly reduces the amount of strain anti-tank weapons place on the region as well as make it easier to develop them. 
Due to this, any destructible object or vehicle is required to use an LBA-compatible health system. These systems ARE required to be open-source due to a long history of people attempting to circumvent region regulations with dubious alterations.

The system was originally conceived by Sin Straaf, however Dread Hudson (aka Krutchen) is mainly credited for its original implementation and development.

# What restrictions are common?
Barring AP cap values, which are roughly 40 to 50% of the object's total HP, most restrictions limit the total amount of HP allowed for objects. Some values include but are not limited to...
- 200 HP for ground vehicles
- 100 HP for static deployables
- 25 or 50 HP for active deployables
- Most anti-tank weapons deal between 35 and 70 LBA damage.

Your usage may vary. Consult region guidelines for what they permit.

# What repos are available for LBA?
